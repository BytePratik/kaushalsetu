KAUSHALSETU (कौशलसेतु)

National Skill Development, Career Planning & Employment Ecosystem

Over 12M students graduate annually, but 47%+ face employability challenges due to a gap between college education and real-world employer skill requirements.

✅ The KAUSHALSETU Solution

Data-driven skill gap measurement, personalized milestone roadmaps, AI career guidance, and verified candidate job matching.
